# Development protocol

Use the fixed stratified training split and no external data. Label/tone sampling compares 20,000 unique training examples, 2,000 per class, at a fixed 40,000-presentation epoch budget. Seeds are 42, 43 and 44. The wider sweep contains eight architecture, twelve data and fourteen training directions; executable registration files specify the complete configurations. The report and evidence in the submission repository describe the historical results and limitations.

This English protocol note is for a fresh reproduction workspace. It does not replace the original frozen experiment records.
